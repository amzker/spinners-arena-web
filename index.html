🛡️ 1. Commitment & Core Principles

Effective date: 6 September 2026. AmzkerStudios ("we", "our") develops and operates Spinners Arena. The game works offline except for advertising and LAN features and does not require an account.

🚫 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Player Account: We do not ask for your real name, phone number, physical address, or email inside the game.
  • No Location Access: Precise GPS and coarse network location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) are permanently disabled.
  • No Private File Access: We never request access to your contacts, camera, photo gallery, or device storage files.

💾 3. Local Game Data Architecture

Career progress, unlocked spinner parts, loadouts, credits, and achievements are stored inside your device's local application sandbox. Clearing app data or uninstalling the game removes this local save.

📢 4. Advertising & InMobi Integration

To fund development and provide optional rewards such as combat intel and workshop credits, Spinners Arena uses the InMobi advertising SDK. When ads are requested or shown, InMobi may process advertising or device identifiers, IP address and city-level location inferred from it, device and network details, app/SDK information, and ad views, clicks, or other interactions. InMobi uses this information to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repetition, prevent fraud, and improve its advertising services.

  • Sensitive Categories Blocked: All Gambling, Political, and Alcohol advertisements are strictly blocked.
  • Third-Party Policy: Review InMobi's Privacy Policy for its current practices, retention, sharing, and privacy request process.
  • Opt-Out Controls: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android's privacy or ads settings. InMobi also provides interest-based advertising opt-out choices in its privacy policy.

👶 5. Age Audience

Spinners Arena is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertisement, contact us and InMobi using the links on this page.

🗑️ 6. User Data Deletion

We do not operate player accounts or a gameplay database. You can delete local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Requests concerning information processed by InMobi can be submitted through InMobi's privacy policy; you may also contact us at the support address below.
